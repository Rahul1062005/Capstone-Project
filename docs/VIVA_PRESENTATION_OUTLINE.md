## 1. Slide-by-Slide Presentation Outline (12 Slides)

### Slide 1 — Title & Context
- **Title:** VoiceShield (`BAFV-PCTA`): AI Deepfake & Vishing Fraud Prevention for Banking Calls
- **Alignment:** SIH `SIH26104` (AICTE Cyber Security Cell — Cybersecurity & FinTech)
- **Scope Disclaimer:** Research prototype providing **advisory** fraud risk signals (`ALLOW`, `VERIFY`, `HOLD_FOR_REVIEW`) to human analysts.

### Slide 2 — The Problem: Executive & Customer Vishing
- Real-time neural TTS and zero-shot voice cloning bypass human ear checks and traditional IVR voiceprints.
- **Three Gaps in Existing Passive Detectors:**
  1. High false alarms on lossy **8 kHz telephone channels** (AMR / G.711 codecs strip energy > 3.4 kHz).
  2. Poor generalization to **unseen generative vocoders** (zero-day cloners).
  3. Zero awareness of **financial transaction risk** (₹500 check vs. ₹5,00,000 urgent wire).

### Slide 3 — Core Innovation: Biomechanical & Physiological Coupling (`BAFV-PCTA`)
- Human speech is physically constrained by subglottal lung pressure, glottal micro-jitter/shimmer, and continuous tongue/jaw formant inertia ($F_1, F_2, F_3$).
- **6 Coupled Trajectory Pairs (`PCVS`):** Measures lagged cross-correlation and Mahalanobis distance from the human speech manifold.
- **PTVA & RPCI:** Flags biologically impossible formant velocities and missing respiration-to-phonation coupling (marking unmeasurable cues as missing, never suspicious).

### Slide 4 — System Architecture (3-Tier Cloud Stack)
- **Frontend Console (React + TypeScript, Vercel):** Analyst workstation with Light/Dark themes, Call Inspector, Risk Timeline, Voice Challenge, and Audit Ledger.
- **Enterprise Gateway (Java 21 Spring Boot, Render Docker):** Proxies audio analysis, generates HMAC-SHA256 challenges, and anchors SHA-256 hash-chained audit logs (`zero raw audio retained`).
- **AI & Signal Engine (Python FastAPI + PyTorch + Librosa, Render Docker):** Audio validation, VAD, spectro-temporal CNN, BAFV-PCTA, OGD, TCS, DRFE, and TB-PC verifier.

### Slide 5 — Streaming Window Analysis & Telephony Confidence (`TCS`)
- Continuous audio analyzed in **2.0 s sliding windows with 0.5 s hop** (`ai_service/windowed_analyzer.py`).
- Exponential risk smoothing: $S_t = 0.6 S_{t-1} + 0.4 s_t$.
- **TCS & Low-Confidence Guard:** Computes channel confidence $C$ from SNR and 8 kHz band-limiting; when $C < 0.5$, voice evidence alone cannot trigger a unilateral `HOLD_FOR_REVIEW` (downgrades to `VERIFY`).

### Slide 6 — Dynamic Risk Fusion Engine (`DRFE`) & Adaptive Threshold
- Logit formulation combining voice spoof score $R_v$, physiological anomaly $P_{\text{pcta}}$, speaker risk $R_s$, channel confidence $C$, transaction risk $R_t$, and cross-interaction $R_v \cdot R_t$.
- **Amount-Aware Threshold $\tau_L(a)$:** Higher transfer amounts automatically tighten verification sensitivity ($\tau_L$ scales down from `0.35` toward `0.21`).
- **Persistence Rule:** Requires `HOLD` band in $\ge 3$ of the last 5 windows unless extreme transaction risk ($R_t > 0.9$).

### Slide 7 — Transaction-Bound Spoken Challenge (`TB-PC`)
- Ephemeral 60-second challenge derived via `HMAC-SHA256(K_bank, TxnID | Amount | Nonce)`.
- Defeats **pre-recorded replay attacks** (wrong digits / `< 350 ms` instant bot trigger) and exposes **on-demand AI TTS synthesis lag** (`> 2.8 s` response delay vs. `0.4–2.8 s` human cadence).

### Slide 8 — Open-Set Generator Detection (`OGD`)
- Dual-head classification: matches known generator cluster centroids (`ElevenLabs`, `RVC-v2`, `Bark`, `HiFi-GAN`, `VALL-E`) or flags unseen architectures as `UNKNOWN_ZERO_DAY_SYNTHETIC`.
- Evaluated via Leave-One-Generator-Out (`LOGO`) protocol.

### Slide 9 — Privacy, Security & Compliance (`FR-11`, `FR-12`, `FR-13`)
- **Zero raw audio stored:** Only extracted feature metadata is hashed into the SHA-256 ledger.
- **DPDP / GDPR Purge (`DELETE /api/audit/records/{id}`):** Wipes identifiable metadata while preserving cryptographic chain hashes.
- **Fail-Secure (`FR-13`):** Corrupt files or unreachable downstream services surface explicit HTTP 422/502 errors—never fabricated scores.

### Slide 10 — Evaluation Protocol & Results (`Phase 7`)

  - Clean 16 kHz EER, min-tDCF, ROC-AUC, Avg Latency

## 3. Top Examiner Questions & Honest Answers

1. **Q: Why use a Spring Boot gateway in front of FastAPI instead of calling FastAPI directly?**  
   *A:* In banking architectures, the AI microservice stays isolated from financial logic. Spring Boot handles cryptographic challenge derivation (`HMAC-SHA256`), tamper-evident SHA-256 audit chaining, multipart validation, and policy orchestration cleanly.
2. **Q: Why doesn't the system freeze accounts automatically on a high spoof score?**  
   *A:* False positives over noisy phone lines hurt real customers. VoiceShield is strictly an advisory decision-support tool (`FR-08`) with a Low-Confidence Guard and 3-of-5 persistence rule so human analysts confirm high-impact holds.
3. **Q: What happens if a brand-new AI voice cloner appears that wasn't in training?**  
   *A:* `OpenSetGeneratorDetector` combines known generator clusters with distance from the genuine human physiological manifold (`PCTA`), flagging out-of-manifold attacks as `UNKNOWN_ZERO_DAY_SYNTHETIC`.
4. **Q: What if `models/aasist_baseline.pt` isn't loaded?**  
   *A:* `/health` and the UI explicitly display `Seed Init` (`weights_source: "untrained_seed_init"`). Training with `notebooks/VoiceShield_Phase7_Train_and_Eval.ipynb` produces `aasist_baseline.pt` and `calibration_stats.json`, which `api.py` loads automatically.
